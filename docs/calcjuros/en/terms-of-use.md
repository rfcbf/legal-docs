## 1. What CalcJuros is

CalcJuros is a financial calculator for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It brings together calculators for simple and compound interest, loan payments, mortgages (Price and SAC), boletos, credit cards, checks, overdraft, receivables discount, total loan cost (CET), investment returns, inflation adjustment, rate conversion, net salary and employee × contractor (CLT × PJ), plus tools to organize a personal book of clients, checks and loans. It follows Brazilian rules, rates and tables.

CalcJuros is a support tool for calculations and personal organization. **It is not a financial institution, does not grant credit, does not broker loans, does not collect payments on your behalf and does not provide financial, accounting, legal or tax advice.**
