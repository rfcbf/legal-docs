# CalcJuros Support

Need help with CalcJuros? Write to **[renatodesenv@icloud.com](mailto:renatodesenv@icloud.com)** with your device model, system version and app version (Settings › About). You can also send suggestions and report issues from the app, in Settings › Feedback.

More about the app at [calcjuros.renatoferraz.net/en](https://calcjuros.renatoferraz.net/en/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an account?

No. Tap **Start without an account** and use the app with your data stored in your iCloud. Sign-in only appears for people importing data from an old account.

### How do I bring my data from the old version?

In Settings, choose **Import data from an old account** and sign in once with the email, Google or Apple account you used before. The app copies calculations, clients, checks and card providers to your iCloud.

### I already bought CalcJuros. Do I have to pay again?

No. The app recognizes your purchase through the App Store receipt, and you keep the calculators, check tracking and iCloud sync. Loans, widgets, Apple Watch, backups and the 5.0 features come with the subscription. If the app doesn't recognize your purchase, use **Restore purchases** with the same Apple account used to buy it.

### My data doesn't show up on another device.

- The devices need to use the same Apple account, with iCloud turned on for CalcJuros.
- Sync can take a few minutes. Opening the app on each device helps fetch the changes.

### The result differs from my bank's.

The calculations use standard market formulas and are meant as a reference. The contract may include fees, insurance and IOF. To compare the full cost, use the **Loan total cost** calculator.

### I didn't get a check due date reminder.

Check that CalcJuros notifications are on in the system Settings and in the app, and that a Focus mode isn't silencing them.

### I forgot my lock PIN.

Use Face ID or Touch ID if they are turned on. The PIN is stored only on the device where it was created.

### How do I cancel the subscription?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › CalcJuros. The subscription stays active until the end of the paid period. Refunds are requested from Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### How do I delete my data?

See "Your rights and control" in the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md). To delete suggestions you sent or your old account's data, write to the email above.
