# PontoCerto Support

Need help with PontoCerto? Write to **[renatodesenv@icloud.com](mailto:renatodesenv@icloud.com)** with your device model, system version and app version (Settings › About). You can also send suggestions and report problems from the app, in Settings › About › Feedback.

## Frequently asked questions

### The alarm didn't ring. What should I check?

- Real alarms are part of PontoCerto Pro. Without Pro, the app notifies you until you confirm the punch.
- Check that the alarm is on in Settings › Alarm and that PontoCerto has Alarms and Notifications permission in the system Settings.
- Focus modes, Low Power Mode and system updates can delay or silence alerts. The app is a support tool: always check the time in your company's official time clock.

### How do I set up the automatic Focus?

Follow the [Set up automatic Focus](focus-guide.md) guide.

### My data doesn't show up on my other device.

- Devices must use the same Apple Account, with iCloud turned on for PontoCerto.
- Sync can take a few minutes. Opening the app on each device helps fetch the changes.

### I subscribed to Pro, but it doesn't show up on another device.

Open Settings › PontoCerto Pro and tap **Restore Purchases**, using the same Apple Account you bought with. The subscription works on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

### How do I cancel the subscription?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › PontoCerto. On Mac: System Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Pro stays active until the end of the period already paid. Refunds are requested from Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### How do I keep my data or move it to another device?

Use Settings › Backup to create a backup file and import it on the other device. With iCloud on, data also syncs automatically.

### How do I delete my data?

See the "Your rights and control" section of the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md). To delete the UAPP account and the suggestions you sent, write to the e-mail above.
