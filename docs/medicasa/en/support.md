# MediCasa Support

Need help with MediCasa? Write to **[renatodesenv@icloud.com](mailto:renatodesenv@icloud.com)** with your device model, system version and app version (Settings › About). You can also send suggestions and report problems in the app, in Settings › About › Feedback.

## Frequently asked questions

### A reminder or alarm did not go off. What should I check?

- Reminders and alarms are part of Premium.
- Check that MediCasa has Notifications and Alarms permission in the system Settings, and that the alarm is on in Settings › Alarms.
- Focus, Do Not Disturb and Low Power Mode can delay or silence alerts. MediCasa is a helper tool: do not rely on it alone for important doses.

### How do I add a medicine from a photo of the box?

In Add › Medicine, tap fill from the box and follow the steps: front of the box, batch and expiry, and barcode. Reading happens on the device; check the data before using it. This feature is part of Premium, and the "What it's for" description needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence.

### How do I share medicines with my family?

In Settings › Home, create a home and invite people with the QR code or the code. Everyone sees the same medicines, reminders and purchases. Family Premium covers everyone in the home.

### I subscribed to Premium, but it doesn't show on another device.

Open Settings › Plans › View plans and tap **Restore purchases**, using the same Apple Account you bought with.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › MediCasa. Premium stays active until the end of the paid period. Refunds are requested from Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, in Profile, tap **Delete account**. See "Your rights and choices" in the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
